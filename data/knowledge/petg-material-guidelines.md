# PETG Material Guidelines

## Storage and moisture

PETG absorbs moisture from the air. Popping sounds, a rough surface, excessive stringing, and inconsistent extrusion are common warning signs. Store opened spools in a sealed container with dry desiccant and dry the material according to the manufacturer's published limit before changing slicer settings.

## Temperature window

The synthetic baseline for Atlas One is 235 to 245 degrees Celsius at the nozzle and 65 to 75 degrees Celsius at the bed. Start from the material profile, then change only one parameter at a time. A higher temperature can improve layer bonding but can also increase stringing and surface artifacts.

## First layer

PETG should be deposited with consistent contact without being over-compressed. Excessive squish can damage coated build surfaces and create an enlarged first layer. Clean the plate with the approved process, verify the Z offset, and use a release layer when required by the surface manufacturer.

## Quality acceptance

Accept the job only when walls are continuous, dimensions remain within the part tolerance, corners stay attached, and stringing does not interfere with function. Record material lot, profile version, machine, and inspection result for repeatable production.
