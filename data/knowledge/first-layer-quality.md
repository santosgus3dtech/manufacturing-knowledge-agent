# First-Layer Quality Standard

## Visual criteria

An accepted first layer has continuous lines, consistent width, attached corners, and no visible nozzle dragging. Gaps indicate insufficient contact or flow, while a translucent or heavily ridged surface can indicate excessive compression.

## Warping response

When corners lift, verify plate cleanliness, bed temperature, ambient drafts, material profile, and part orientation before adding adhesive or increasing temperature. Change one variable at a time and preserve the failed sample or image for comparison.

## Dimensional check

Measure the defined coupon after cooling. The result must remain inside the profile tolerance and should be compared with the last accepted calibration. Do not compensate for dimensional error by scaling a production model until the source of the deviation is understood.

## Traceability

Record machine, plate, material lot, profile version, measured dimensions, visual result, and disposition. A pass without the profile version is not reproducible evidence.
