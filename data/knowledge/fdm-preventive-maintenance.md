# FDM Preventive Maintenance Guide

## Daily inspection

Before the first job, inspect the build surface for residue, confirm that the nozzle is visually clear, and verify that filament travels without sharp bends. A machine reporting abnormal vibration should remain idle until the motion system is checked. Record observations before changing tension or alignment.

## Extrusion path

Intermittent under-extrusion can come from a partially blocked nozzle, a worn drive gear, excessive spool resistance, or wet material. Heat the nozzle only to the approved material range, unload safely, inspect the filament bite pattern, and use the documented cleaning procedure. Do not increase temperature repeatedly to hide a mechanical restriction.

## Motion system

Unexpected ringing or vibration requires a powered-off visual inspection of belts, pulleys, fasteners, and carriage movement. Belt tension should be even, not maximized. After maintenance, run the synthetic calibration model and compare the result with the previous accepted sample before returning the machine to production.

## Maintenance record

Every intervention should record machine id, symptom, observation, action, operator, timestamp, and validation result. Replaced components and changed settings belong in the immutable maintenance log so later incidents can be compared against the same configuration.
