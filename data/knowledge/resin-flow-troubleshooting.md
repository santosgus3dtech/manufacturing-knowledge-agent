# Resin Flow Troubleshooting

## Stop condition

If the resin circulation or refill check reports restricted flow, pause production planning for the cell. Do not start another exposure cycle until the vat, resin temperature, and feed path have been inspected. Keep uncured resin handling inside the documented protective-equipment process.

## Inspection sequence

Confirm that the vat contains enough resin, the material is within its specified temperature range, and no cured fragment blocks movement. Inspect the feed path for a closed valve, kink, or settled material. Never scrape the film with a metal tool, and filter resin when contamination is suspected.

## Recovery validation

After the obstruction is removed, run the short synthetic flow verification and inspect the vat before loading a production file. A successful sensor reading alone is not sufficient when fragments were found. Document the observation, filtered volume, material batch, and validation outcome.

## Escalation

Escalate when restriction returns after one verified cleaning cycle, when the film is marked, or when a sensor disagrees with a manual inspection. Keep the machine unavailable until the cause and the post-maintenance test are recorded.
